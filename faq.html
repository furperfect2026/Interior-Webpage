Interior Design Queries in Pune

Everything you need to know before starting your dream home or commercial space.

What is the average 2 BHK interior design cost in Pune?

The interior design cost in Pune generally starts around ₹1,200 - ₹1,650 per sq. ft. for budget finishes, while premium luxury bespoke interiors can range from ₹3,000 to over ₹5,500 per sq. ft.

For a standard 2 BHK in areas like Dhanori or Viman Nagar, comprehensive turnkey interiors typically range between ₹5 Lakhs to ₹12 Lakhs, depending on materials, smart home integration, and custom factory-made modular fittings.

Why choose factory-made furniture over local carpenters?

At Raza Enterprises, our 100% in-house factory ensures precision manufacturing. Factory-made interiors offer superior edge-banding, perfect alignment, premium machine finishes, and significantly faster delivery times compared to traditional on-site carpentry.

This eliminates the mess, noise, and hidden costs commonly associated with local carpenters, ensuring your home in Pune gets a flawless, modern finish.

What are the trending interior design styles in Dhanori and Pune?

Homeowners in Pune are actively searching for "biophilic design" (bringing nature indoors with terracotta and oak wood tones), smart home automation, and "fusion decor" which blends traditional Indian aesthetics with sleek modern minimalism. Vibrant, expressive colors are also making a major comeback.

What is included in a Turnkey Interior Contract?

A turnkey project means we handle absolutely everything from bare shell to final handover. This includes 3D space planning, civil work, false ceilings, plumbing, electricals, custom factory furniture, painting, and deep cleaning. You simply unlock the door and move in.
