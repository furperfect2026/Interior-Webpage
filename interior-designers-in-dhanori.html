Frequently Asked Questions by Dhanori Residents

Where is your Dhanori factory located and can I visit?

Yes, absolutely! Our production factory is located right on Dhanori-Lohegaon Road. We encourage all prospective clients to visit our workshop to see live German edge-banding machines, raw ply quality, and finishes before signing any contract.

How do you ensure 45-day on-time handover in Dhanori?

Because our factory is 100% in-house, your modular boards, carcasses, and shutters are precision-cut and finished off-site while false ceilings and electrical work happen on site. This parallel processing cuts execution time in half compared to traditional carpenters.

Do you provide interior design services in Lohegaon, Tingre Nagar, and Vishrantwadi?

Yes! While our central factory is based in Dhanori, we regularly execute turnkey interiors across Vishrantwadi, Lohegaon, Tingre Nagar, Airport Road, and Vadgaon Sheri with zero extra transport charges.
