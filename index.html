Why Homeowners Across Pune & Dhanori Choose Raza Enterprises

Unlike traditional interior agencies that outsource woodwork to third-party local carpenters, Raza Enterprises operates a 100% in-house manufacturing factory in Dhanori, Pune. We eliminate 30% to 40% middleman markups and deliver machine-crafted modular furniture with millimeter precision.

01

Direct-From-Factory Advantage

Complete end-to-end control. Our automated German machinery cuts, edge-bands, and presses premium marine ply and HDHMR without bubbling, chipping, or messy on-site carpentry delays.

02

45-Day Handover & 10-Yr Warranty

We respect your time. With prefabricated modular components assembled on site, standard 2 BHK and 3 BHK turnkey projects are installed and handed over in as fast as 45 days, backed by a comprehensive 10-year warranty.

03

Transparent Per Sq Ft Cost

No surprise billing. From budget modular kitchens starting at ₹1.5 Lakhs to complete turnkey luxury 2 BHK & 3 BHK flats starting at ₹5.5 Lakhs in Dhanori and Viman Nagar, every square foot is itemized in 3D before work begins.
